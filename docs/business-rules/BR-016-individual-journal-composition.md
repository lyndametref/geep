# BR-016: Individual Journal Composition

## Description

Individual journals are chronological and include observation entries, future-event entries, treatment details, quarantine delays, attachment references, and medical analysis results.

## Rationale

Based on requirements REQ-04 — Observations and Reproductive Planning, and REQ-13 — Interventions and Care Management, this rule defines the journal as the unified chronological view of an individual's full history. It aggregates all Record subtypes (Observation, Intervention, FutureEvent and its specializations) sorted by timestamp, providing the shepherd with a single timeline of everything affecting that animal.

## Applicability

Applies whenever an individual's journal is viewed. The journal includes all Record entries linked to the individual, ordered chronologically. Filtering by entry type may be provided in the UI.
