# REQ-14.003 The system generates profit/loss reports

## User Story
As a shepherd, I want to generate profit/loss reports so that I can assess the financial health of my exploitation over a given period.

## Group
REQ-14 Financial Tracking

## Criticality
Could have

## Description
The system must generate basic profit/loss reports for a given date range, showing total income, total expenditure, and net profit or loss. The report must be exportable or viewable in a human-readable format.

## Acceptance Criteria
- The system generates a profit/loss report for a configurable date range.
- The report displays total income, total expenditure, and net result.
- Income and expenditure breakdowns by category are included.
- The report can be exported or viewed on screen.
