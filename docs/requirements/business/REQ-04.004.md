# REQ-04.004 A mating observation produces a birth planned event at a range of 140 to 150 days after observation.

## User Story
As a shepherd, I want mating observations to automatically generate expected birth planning events so that reproductive follow-up is timely.

## Group
REQ-04 Observations and Reproductive Planning

## Criticality
Should have

## Description
A mating observation must produce a planned birth event in a window of 140 to 150 days after the observation date.

## Acceptance Criteria
- When a mating observation is recorded, the system generates a planned event for birth.
- The birth planned event date is between 140 and 150 days after the mating observation date.
