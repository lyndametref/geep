# REQ-13 Interventions and Care Management

## User Story
As a shepherd, I want to record interventions and plan care activities so that I can track treatments, prevention, and routine management.

## Description
The system must record interventions such as treatments, shearing, and hoof trimming. It must support batch entry for interventions. It must track quarantine periods and care protocols. It must provide reminders for routine care activities.It must also keep a chronological journal of observation and intervention for each individual that can be easily consulted by the shepherd. For observation see [REQ-04](REQ-04.md).
