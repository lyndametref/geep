# REQ-13.005 The journal stores intervention details.

## User Story
As a shepherd, I want intervention details and quarantine periods to be visible in the journal so that I can follow care and treatment protocols safely.

## Group
[REQ-13 Interventions and Care Management](REQ-13.md)

## Criticality
Should have

## Description
The journal must store intervention details including intervention date, dose (for treatment interventions), and quarantine periods.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Journal entries for interventions include the intervention date.
- Journal entries for treatment interventions include the administered dose.
- Journal entries for treatment interventions trigger the creation of quarantine periods.
