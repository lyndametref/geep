# REQ-04 Observations and Automated Events

As a sheperd, I want to record observations and trigger future events so that I can maintain health and reproduction traceability.

The system must record observations related to weight, health, and reproduction. It must support batch entry for observations and treatments. It must derive reminders and lifecycle events from recorded observations. It must also keep a chronological journal for each individual that can be easily consulted by the sheep owner.
