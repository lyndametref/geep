# REQ-04 Observations and Reproductive Planning

As a shepherd, I want to record observations and plan reproductive follow-up so that I can maintain health and reproduction traceability.

The system must record observations related to weight, health, and reproduction events. It must support batch entry for observations. It must derive reminders and lifecycle events from recorded observations. It must also keep a chronological journal of observation and intervention for each individual that can be easily consulted by the sheep owner. For intervention recording and care management, see REQ-13 Interventions and Care Management.
